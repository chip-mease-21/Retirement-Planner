Three Tax Strategies Worth Discussing with Your CPA
1. State of residence — $12K/yr decision Virginia taxes your retirement income at 5.75%. Florida, Tennessee, and Texas have zero state income tax. At your income level, moving saves ~$12,292/year — $245,848 over 20 years. If the kids land somewhere warm, this deserves a deliberate conversation before retirement.
2. Eric should consider Roth contributions Eric's entire 403(b) is pre-tax — every dollar he withdraws is taxable income. Karen is smart to split 50/50. Ask Guidestone/Empower if a Roth 403(b) option is available. Converting even $500/mo to Roth now reduces future taxable income. Your current effective rate (16.4%) ≈ retirement rate (16.3%) — so now is a neutral time to convert.
3. Housing allowance sizing matters The larger your documented housing expenses in retirement, the more you can designate as housing allowance and exclude from tax. If you buy a smaller home outright (the $400K cash option), your allowable housing expenses drop. If you rent, your rent qualifies. Plan your retirement housing around maximizing this exclusion — it's worth $1,000+/month in tax savings.
